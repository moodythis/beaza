Small payments add up quietly

Coffee between lectures, a late delivery order, a taxi home. Each one is a dinar or two on a card or BenefitPay, and none of it feels like spending in the moment. The month-end total does. Beaza puts a number on today, while there is still time to act on it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Your bank texts you

    Pay by card or Fawri+ as usual. Your bank already sends an SMS for each transaction.

  2. 2

    Beaza reads it on your phone

    Amount, merchant, type and time are extracted on the device. If Beaza is not sure of a value, it leaves it blank rather than guess.

  3. 3

    Your day updates

    What's left of today's limit updates at once, with alerts at 50%, 80% and 100%.

A calmer way to spend

  1. 1

    Notice

    Every payment moves the figure for what is left today, so small spending shows up while it happens.

  2. 2

    Choose

    Before you tap pay, check whether it fits today, or let it wait a day.

  3. 3

    Reset

    At the end of the day, unspent money can move to your goal, and tomorrow starts with a fresh limit.

Questions people ask

Does it read Arabic SMS?

Not yet. Every transaction alert we have collected so far is in English, so Beaza reads English alerts today. The app itself is fully Arabic.

Does Beaza need my bank login?

No. It only reads the SMS your bank already sends to your phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. Beaza is Android only for now, because it reads SMS on the phone.

What happens when Beaza is unsure?

It leaves the field blank and puts the message on a short review list, instead of guessing.

Your money data stays yours

Beaza works without an account. If you choose to sync between phones, records are encrypted on your device first, so our server stores data it cannot read.
