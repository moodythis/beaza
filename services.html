Everything you need to keep today on budget

Beaza is one app with a small set of services that work together, from the moment a bank SMS arrives to the end of your day.

Automatic expense capture

Beaza reads transaction SMS from your bank and wallets and records the amount, merchant, type and time. Messages it cannot read go to a short review list, so nothing is silently lost.

A daily spending limit

Tell Beaza your income, fixed commitments and how much you want to save. It works out a daily limit in exact fils and recalculates it after every change.

Timely alerts and a daily close

Alerts at 50%, 80% and 100% of the limit. At the end of the day, unspent money can move to your savings goal.

Impulse control

A "Can I buy it?" check against today's limit, a 24-hour hold for things you want but do not need yet, and weekly tips.

Ask about your spending

Type a question such as "How much did I spend this week?" in Arabic or English. The phone answers from your own records. An optional "Smarter answers" mode can word the reply better, and any reply with a number Beaza did not supply is thrown away.

Encrypted sync between phones

Optional. Records are encrypted on your phone before they leave it, so the server only ever stores data it cannot open.

Currency conversion

Spent in dollars, riyals or dirhams? Beaza converts USD, SAR, AED and QAR to dinars at their fixed pegs on the phone, and KWD at a dated, approximate rate.
