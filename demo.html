Example data only. This is an English-only web prototype, not the Android app: it does not read SMS, connect to a bank or save anything you enter. The Android app runs fully in Arabic and English.

Install on Android

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks. Beaza is not on Google Play yet, so Google Play Protect may warn you or block the install. If it does, choose "Install anyway", or pause Play Protect scanning for the install and turn it back on afterwards.
  3. Allow SMS access when Beaza asks. It reads English-language alerts from supported banks, on the phone.
  4. Enter your income and commitments, and your daily limit appears.
