Install on Android

  1. Download the APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it and allow installs from this source when Android asks. Because Beaza is not on Google Play yet, Android may show a warning.
  3. Allow SMS access when Beaza asks, so it can read your bank messages on the phone.
  4. Enter your income and commitments, and your daily limit appears.
