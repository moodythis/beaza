Built for accuracy, privacy and everyday use

A closer look at what Beaza does for you, how it works under the hood, and who it is for.

Benefits

  • See today's spending at a glance, not at the end of the month.
  • Stop typing in expenses: the SMS your bank sends is enough.
  • Get a nudge at the moment it helps, before the next purchase.
  • Turn the money you did not spend into progress toward a goal.

Technical capabilities

On-device extraction

Twenty rule-based templates for seven Bahraini banks and wallets. The extractor leaves a field blank rather than guess.

Exact money maths

Every amount is stored and computed in integer fils, so there are no rounding surprises.

Strong encryption

Local database encrypted on the phone. Sync uses XChaCha20-Poly1305 with Argon2id key derivation.

Checked AI answers

The phone computes every figure. An optional model only words the answer, and replies with unchecked numbers are discarded.

Light, serverless backend

Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database held in the EU jurisdiction.

Tested on every change

More than 1,100 automated tests run on every update, across the extractor, assistant, encryption, server and app.

Who it is for

University students in Bahrain who pay mostly by card, BenefitPay or Fawri+, and anyone with a fixed allowance or salary who wants a daily number instead of a monthly surprise.

Why Beaza

No typing

Manual trackers depend on a habit people tend to drop. Beaza starts from messages you already receive.

Many banks, one view

Reads messages from several Bahraini banks and wallets, with no bank login or open-banking licence needed.

Daily, not monthly

A limit for today is something you can act on before the next purchase.

Local and bilingual

Made for Bahraini banks, dinars and fils, with an app you can use in Arabic or English.
