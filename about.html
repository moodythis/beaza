We build money tools for how students actually pay.

Beaza started with a simple observation: cash is rare in a Bahraini student's day, and card spending is easy to lose track of. We set out to turn the messages banks already send into a clear daily number.

Mission

Help students see what each day costs and stay inside a limit they chose, without typing in a single expense.

Vision

A Bahrain where every young person knows where their money goes, and their financial data stays under their own control.

Objectives

  1. Read the SMS formats of the main Bahraini banks and wallets accurately, and never record a value we are unsure of.
  2. Turn income, commitments and savings into a daily limit, and alert at the right moments.
  3. Keep processing on the phone by default, and ask for consent before any network feature.
  4. Offer tools that help with impulse buying: a 24-hour hold, a daily close and weekly tips.
  5. Offer the whole app in Arabic and English, and add Arabic-language bank SMS next.

The team

Two computer science students at the University of Bahrain who designed, built and tested Beaza together.
